Both sides need to submit an offer before you can compare totals.
Nothing has moved yet at this point — submitting an offer only records your items on-chain and approves the contract to move them. When both sides confirm, "Execute swap" makes one call that transfers every item on both sides inside a single transaction. If any single transfer would fail — a missing approval, an item that changed hands, a revoked allowance — the entire transaction reverts and nobody's items move. Approvals stay live until you revoke them or the contract does, so cancelling a trade you no longer want is worth doing explicitly rather than just walking away.
